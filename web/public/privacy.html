Privacy policy

Last updated: 7 October 2026

Everything runs in your browser. No account, no analytics, no cookies, no ads.

  • Your progress is a file you save yourself – on your computer or, only if you choose so, in your own Google Drive. It holds your playthroughs, checked items and bestiary entries, and only the names of your world and character.
  • World and player files are read in your browser and never uploaded.
  • Your browser keeps a backup of the open file and your view settings. Clear the site's data to remove them.
  • Requests: the app is served by GitHub Pages (which may log your IP address), and a few images come from terraria.wiki.gg. Nothing else is sent to anyone.

Google Drive (optional)

If you choose it, you sign in with Google and grant the permission drive.file: the app can only see the files it created itself. It saves your progress file in a folder "Terraria Progress Tracker" in your Drive, keeps the access in memory only and your account's address on your device. Delete the files in the app or in Drive; revoke the access in your Google account. The app's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
